Privacy policy: rclone nasT20

Last updated: 2026-10-19

rclone nasT20 is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

  • It accesses the owner's own Google Drive, using the owner's own sign-in, from the owner's own home server.
  • It uploads files the owner chooses, and reads files the owner already has.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Security → Third-party connections.
